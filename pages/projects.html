No. 01Sewage treatment

A 100 KLD sewage plant for Hyline Enviro, in Egypt

Our only installed plant outside India on the credentials list, and one of the four largest at 100 KLD.

Client
Hyline Enviro
Location
Egypt
Capacity
100 KLD (100,000 litres a day)
Supplied
Sewage treatment plant
Process
Electro-coagulation and ozonation
Status
Active / running

The brief

Hyline Enviro needed a plant for 100 KLD of sewage at a site in Egypt, a long way from our works in Ankleshwar.

What we built

Our sewage plants work by electro-coagulation: an aluminium-cell reactor applies an electric field to the raw sewage so the suspended matter comes together, ozone then disinfects it, a tube settler takes out the sludge and a carbon filter polishes the water. No chemicals are dosed and there is no aeration tank.

The plant is skid-mounted and modular, with a small footprint, and needs less civil construction than a conventional plant. It starts and stops when the operator chooses.

Where it stands

The plant is listed as active and running in our installed-plant credentials.

No. 05Scrubbing systems

Scrubbers on a skid: columns, pumps and fan on one frame

Twin scrubbing columns with their recirculation pumps and blower, assembled and piped on a single base frame in our shop.

Client
Not named
Supplied
Scrubbing columns, recirculation pumps, centrifugal blower, interconnecting piping, base frame
Materials
Thermoplastic columns and piping

The brief

Exhaust from a process has to be cleaned of gases and particles before it leaves the plant. A scrubber does that, but it only works as a system: columns, liquid recirculation, a fan, and the ducting between them.

What we built

We built the columns, pumps and blower as one unit on a common frame, piped together in our shop. The compact version stands in its own tray.

We call the compact version our smallest yet smartest scrubber system.

Where it stands

Because the whole system is built and piped on one frame, it leaves our works as a unit rather than as loose parts. We supply them as turnkey units, with the fans, mist eliminators and ducting they need.

No. 06Scrubbing systems · Ducting & piping

Big scrubbers and their ducting, built as a set

Horizontal and vertical scrubbers, with the ducting that feeds them, built to work as one system.

Client
Not named
Supplied
Horizontal and vertical scrubbers, ducting, centrifugal fans
Materials
FRP and PVC

The brief

Large exhaust volumes need large scrubbers, and the scrubber is only part of the job: the gas has to reach it and leave it through ducting, all in materials that the fumes cannot corrode.

What we built

We make scrubbers both ways round. Vertical columns stand tall and are lifted into place by crane; horizontal scrubbers lie along the ground, with the fan built on as one combination.

The ducting and bends are made in the same corrosion-resistant materials, and we assemble ducts in our shop to cut installation time in the field.

Where it stands

We supply scrubbing systems as turnkey units, with the fans, mist eliminators and ducting they need.

No. 07Tanks & vessels

Fewer joints: a large tank shell rolled from one sheet

Fewer joints mean fewer places for a tank to fail.

Client
Not named
Supplied
Large thermoplastic tanks and vessels
Materials
Thermoplastic sheet, extrusion-welded

The brief

Every joint in a large non-metallic tank is a place where it could one day leak or need maintenance. On a big shell there are normally many of them.

What we built

We engineer large equipment to cut out unnecessary joints wherever the design permits. Here a large sheet is rolled into the shell, so the shell is made from fewer, larger pieces and needs fewer seams.

The seams that remain are extrusion-welded on German Wegener and Widos equipment, to DVS 2207 and 2208.

Where it stands

Fewer joints mean better corrosion resistance, fewer points to maintain and reliable long-term performance.

The brief

Pickling tanks are used to treat metal surfaces: the metal is dipped in pickling acid to take off scale and rust. The tanks are long and open, and the acid attacks steel.
