Our story

From one shed to the world's demanding plants.

Plasto-Fab began in 1992 in Ankleshwar, Gujarat, founded by Mr. Mehul Pandya, a DVS-certified plastic welder. Work started in our first shed in Ankleshwar GIDC, with 12,000 sq ft of manufacturing space.

Jhannu Pandya represents the second generation of Plasto-Fab, carrying the business forward on the principles it was founded on.

Today Plasto-Fab is recognised as a technically advanced, quality-driven manufacturer. We design, engineer, manufacture, erect and commission chemical process equipment and piping in reinforced plastics such as GRP, and GRP-reinforced PVC, PP, PVDF, FEP and PFA.

We are also the go-to choice for field service, repair and installation, wherever the site is. Repeat orders from most of our clients are the best proof we know of the quality we deliver.

What we do

From the first sketch to the running plant.

01 · Design

Fabrication drawings, or yours.

We make the design and fabrication drawings ourselves, or work from the drawings you supply.

02 · Engineering

For your loads and your chemicals.

Each vessel is designed for the dynamic and hydrostatic loads of its process, in the material that suits the medium it will hold.

03 · Manufacturing

Welded to DVS standards.

Sheets are cut, bent, rolled and welded in our Ankleshwar works to DVS 2207–2208, on German Wegener & Widos equipment.

04 · Erection

On your site.

We deliver the equipment to your plant and erect it there, setting each piece in place.

05 · Commissioning

And field service after.

We commission the system with your team, and come back for field service, repair and installation wherever the site is.
