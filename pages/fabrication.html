Our philosophy

Fewer joints.
Greater reliability.

We engineer large non-metallic equipment to cut out unnecessary joints wherever the design allows. Each joint we avoid is one less place for a leak to start or for maintenance to be needed.

Combining advanced fabrication technology with our FRP and thermoplastic experience, we build equipment for better corrosion resistance, fewer maintenance points and reliable long-term performance.

How a piece of equipment is made

01 · Material in motion

Skilled hands, stronger solutions.

Engineering thermoplastic sheet, chosen for the chemical it will hold, is cut and handled by a team with three decades of practice.

02 · Shaping the form

Rolled, bent and fitted.

Precision fabrication for real-world performance, on imported bending and butt-welding machines. We work from your drawings, or make the fabrication drawings ourselves.

03 · Seamless welds

Welded to the standard.

Seams are joined by online extrusion welding to DVS 2207–2208, on German Wegener and Widos equipment. Assembly brings every fabricated section together into its final form.

04 · Final inspection

Tested before it leaves.

Every piece of equipment is checked and tested for the conditions it will face, before it leaves our facility.

05 · Delivered & installed

Erected and commissioned.

We deliver, erect and commission on site, and return for field service, repair and installation, wherever the plant is.

Standards & equipment

German standards, imported machines.

DVS 2207–2208

The German Welding Society's guidelines for welding thermoplastics and for the machines that do it. Our welds follow them, for long-life joints.

Wegener & Widos

All our fabrication is done with German-made Wegener and Widos plastic welding equipment and technology.

Online extrusion welding

Going a step further, we use online extrusion for extrusion welding.
